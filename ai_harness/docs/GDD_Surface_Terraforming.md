# Game Design Document: Surface Terraforming Keeper

## 1. Vision
**Core Concept:** A sci-fi twist on the "Dungeon Keeper" formula. Instead of an underground dungeon, the player manages a "Safe Zone" on a hostile alien planet covered in hyper-aggressive, sentient flora. You expand not by digging rock, but by **clearing biomass** and terraforming the surface to support your biomechanical colony.

**Thematic Hook:** "Man vs. Nature" on an interstellar scale. The jungle isn't just an obstacle; it's a living enemy that tries to reclaim cleared ground.

## 2. Core Gameplay Loop
1.  **Expansion:** Send "Bio-Engineers" (Imps) to clear adjacent "Vines" and "Biomass" tiles.
2.  **Infrastructure:** Construct "Habitats" (Rooms) on cleared ground to process resources and house units.
3.  **Defense:** Build "Environmental Inhibitors" (Traps) and deploy "Defense Drones" (Troops) to stop the jungle from regrowing into your base.
4.  **Survival:** Defend against "Xenofauna" (Enemies) that emerge from the untamed parts of the map.

## 3. Key Mechanics

### A. The "Clearing" Mechanic (The Digging)
*   The map is initially covered in **Vines** (Soft wall) and **Ancient Biomass** (Hard wall).
*   Clearing a tile consumes **Energy** and reveals the underlying terrain (Clear ground, Mineral Deposit, or Geothermal Vent).
*   **Strata? No.** Since this is a surface game, we focus on **Flora Density** and **Geological Stability** rather than underground rock layers.

### B. The Sparse World Map
*   The world is an infinite/expanding 2D grid.
*   New tiles are procedurally generated as the player approaches the "Boundary".
*   State is managed via a Sparse Grid (`Map<string, CellData>`) to keep memory low.

### C. Resource Systems
*   **Energy:** Generated by tapping Geothermal Vents. Used for all terraforming actions.
*   **Biomatter:** Collected by clearing vines. Used for "growing" units and organic structures.

## 4. Technical Alignment (Existing Code)
*   **Terrain Types:** `Vines`, `Biomass`, `Clear`, `HabitableZone` (Matches `src/world/Map.ts`).
*   **Engine:** TypeScript/Browser-based with a custom sparse grid implementation.
*   **UI:** CRT-terminal aesthetic (Terminal green, high-contrast, glitch effects).

## 5. Why the Previous "Researcher" Failed
The previous research phase drifted into "Geological Strata" because the harness was not aware of the **Surface/Flora** focus already established in the `src` folder. It interpreted "Dungeon Keeper" literally (underground digging) instead of metaphorically (clearing space). Future research must prioritize:
1.  **Procedural Flora Growth Algorithms** (for the regrowing jungle).
2.  **Pathfinding on Dynamic Grids** (as paths open and close due to growth).
3.  **Canvas Performance** for high-density particle effects (spores, fog of war).
